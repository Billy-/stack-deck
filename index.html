A few nice-to-knows

Your deck and place are saved on this device when browser storage is available. Nothing is sent to a server by the app.

After the app reports “Ready offline”, you can play without a connection. Add it to your home screen from your browser’s Share or menu button.

Keyboard: right arrow for the next card; left arrow for Back. Screen-awake and full-screen support depend on your browser.
